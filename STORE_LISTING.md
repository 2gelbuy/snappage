## Detailed Description

Capture full-page, visible area, or selected region screenshots with one click. Edit instantly with a built-in free editor. Export as PNG, JPEG, or PDF. 100% private - everything stays in your browser.

FREE FEATURES (no account, no limits):

- Full Page Capture - scroll-and-stitch technology captures entire web pages, no matter how long
- Visible Area Capture - screenshot exactly what you see
- Area Selection - draw a rectangle to capture any region
- Built-in Editor - crop, arrows, rectangles, text annotations, blur/redact sensitive data
- PDF Export - save any screenshot as a PDF document
- Smart Auto-Naming - files named {domain}_{title}_{date} automatically
- Multiple Formats - PNG (lossless), JPEG (smaller), PDF
- Keyboard Shortcut - Alt+Shift+S for instant capture
- Dark Mode Editor - easy on the eyes for long editing sessions

PRIVACY FIRST:

- Zero data collection. No analytics. No tracking.
- All processing happens locally in your browser
- Your screenshots never leave your device
- No account required. No sign-up. No cloud.
- Minimal permissions - only what's needed to capture and save

BETTER THAN THE ALTERNATIVES:

- GoFullPage charges $1/month for basic editing. Ours is free.
- FireShot charges $40+ for annotations. Ours is free.
- Awesome Screenshot uploads to their servers. We don't.
- Nimbus requires an account. We don't.

PERFECT FOR:

- Developers & QA engineers - capture bug reports with annotations
- Designers - screenshot entire pages for review
- Students & researchers - save articles as PDFs
- Content creators - capture and annotate for tutorials
- Privacy-conscious users - nothing leaves your browser
