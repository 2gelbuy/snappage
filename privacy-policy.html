Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Full Page Screenshot Master is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the extension collects (spoiler: none).

Data Collection

Full Page Screenshot Master does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Period.

  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No cookies
  • No user accounts or registration
  • No server-side processing
  • No data sent to any third party

How It Works

All screenshots are captured and processed entirely on your device using browser APIs. Your screenshots never leave your browser. The extension:

  • Uses chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab to capture the visible area of your active tab
  • Uses chrome.scripting.executeScript to scroll the page for full-page captures
  • Stitches images using OffscreenCanvas in the service worker
  • Opens an editor tab for annotation — all processing happens locally
  • Uses chrome.downloads to save files to your computer

Permissions Explained

  • activeTab — capture the currently active tab when you click the extension
  • scripting — inject scroll commands for full-page capture and area selection overlay
  • downloads — save screenshots to your computer
  • tabs — read the page title and URL for auto-naming screenshots
  • storage — save your preferences (format, quality, capture mode) locally

Third-Party Services

The extension uses zero third-party services. No APIs, no servers, no cloud storage. Everything runs in your browser.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Given our commitment to zero data collection, changes are unlikely.
